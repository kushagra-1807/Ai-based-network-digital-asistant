Design direction for the dashboard

Subject: a monitor and question-answering assistant for a small network. One person reads it on a laptop when something feels wrong, or an evaluator reads it in a lab.

Made of: three measured series (throughput, latency, loss), a table of devices, alerts with a start and an end, and a typed conversation.

Colours (colour is used for status only):
ground #edf0f1, ink #151c20, mute #566269, rule #c8cfd2,
good #2b7a4b, warning #935500, fault #b3261e.
Contrast on the ground: ink 15.0, mute 5.5, good 4.6, warning 5.2, fault 5.7.
Buttons are outlined in ink. The one main button per area is solid ink.

Type: Georgia for headings, the state sentence and the conversation. Consolas, Menlo or DejaVu Sans Mono for every measured value, with tabular figures. All are installed on Windows, macOS and most Linux, so the page needs no download and works offline.

Leads with: one sentence that says what is wrong, or that nothing is. Then the download figure against the link capacity, clearly larger than the rest, and the other readings in a divided strip.

Structure: sections separated by thin rules and space. No cards.

Refuses: gradients, glow, glass, cards, pills, tracked capitals, emoji, pulsing indicators, legends where a line can carry its own label, chat bubbles, dark navy with cyan.
